\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This report focuses on the legacy signal layer of a quantitative platform
tailored to the Moroccan market, and on how signal outputs are rendered in the
operational dashboard.

The analyzed solution is the original legacy \texttt{/signals} page backed by an
A-G filtering pipeline: candidate generation, out-of-sample walk-forward
evaluation, robustness scoring, redundancy reduction, and weighted ensemble.

The document details three elements: the signal detection methodology, the
legacy frontend implementation, and the dashboard representation model
(aggregate score, family decomposition, labels and sorting views).

Main contribution is a traceable ``data -> robust signal -> dashboard'' chain,
usable before full strategy/backtest integration.

\textbf{Keywords:} Signal Engine, Out-of-Sample, Robustness, Dashboard,
Moroccan Market.
