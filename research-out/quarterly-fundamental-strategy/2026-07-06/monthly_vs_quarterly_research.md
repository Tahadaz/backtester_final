# Monthly vs. Quarterly Research Design Comparison (Phase 7)

Given `quarterly_bm_comparison.md` and `information_refresh_analysis.md` establish that (a) the currently-canonical signal is already quarterly-blended, and (b) an artificial annual-only ablation is nearly indistinguishable from it, **R1 (current monthly formation) and R2 (quarterly information-event formation) would be built from materially the same fundamental information** — the question that remains genuinely open is R3 (fixed calendar-quarter rebalance), which is a *rebalance-frequency* question, not a signal-construction question. That comparison is executed directly at the strategy/realized-return level in `monthly_vs_quarterly_strategy.md`, since re-deriving a full separate predictive-IC study (R1 vs R2 vs R3) would just reproduce the already-reported B/M IC numbers (6m IC ≈0.170) against a numerator that differs for only 5 of 71 symbols and by one refresh event each — not a meaningful new predictive-research question given the evidence gathered.

**Not built this session**: a formal R2 "event-driven formation" IC table (number of genuine information events, per-event IC). Given only 5 symbols would ever have an event that changes the formation instance versus R1, and no restatement of "number of formations" would materially differ from R1's, this was judged not to add information beyond what `quarterly_bm_comparison.md` already quantifies directly (the refresh-event counts *are* the R1-vs-R2 information-event answer).
