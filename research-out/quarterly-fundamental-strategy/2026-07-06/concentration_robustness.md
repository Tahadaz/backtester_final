# Concentration Robustness (Phase 11)

Rebuilt S1 (B/M) holdings and reran the full monthly six-vintage engine with specific symbols removed from the eligible universe entirely (not just underweighted), using the same trusted-universe rules otherwise.

| Universe | N periods | Sharpe | Mean monthly return | Cumulative return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full trusted universe (baseline) | 51 | 1.62 | 2.63% | +220% |
| **Excluding ADH, ADI, JET** (the 3 highest-exposure names from the prior session's attribution) | 51 | **1.45** | 1.92% | **+99%** |
| Excluding full real-estate/construction cluster (ADH, ADI, JET, ARD, RDS) | 51 | **1.36** | 1.70% | **+84%** |

## Is this a broad value effect or a concentrated sector recovery bet?

**Both, honestly** — neither extreme. Removing the three highest-exposure names roughly **halves the cumulative return** (220% → 99%), confirming they were a material driver of the total wealth generated. But the strategy **remains solidly positive with a respectable Sharpe (1.45, then 1.36 after removing the whole 5-name real-estate/construction cluster)** — it does not collapse to zero or go negative. **The correct characterization: B/M identified a genuine, broad cross-sectional value effect, which happened to be amplified by a real-estate/construction sector recovery over this specific period — removing that cluster leaves a smaller but still credible value effect, not a null result.**
