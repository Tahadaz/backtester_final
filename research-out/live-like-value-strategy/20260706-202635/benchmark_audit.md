# Benchmark Audit (Phase 10)

`market_data_store` has a genuine `MASI` price index series (`market_data/symbols/MASI/ohlcv.parquet`), loaded and used directly — not fabricated, not substituted with the equal-weight research universe. **Caveat, stated not hidden**: this is confirmed to be the **MASI price index**, not independently verified here to be a total-return series — Moroccan MASI total-return history was not sourced this session (no reliable free total-return series was located in the time available), so the benchmark comparison in `strategy_results.md` likely understates the benchmark's true total return by whatever dividend yield MASI constituents pay. This makes the strategy's apparent outperformance vs. MASI a **conservative-to-generous** comparison in the strategy's favor (a fair total-return benchmark would be a harder hurdle) — flagged explicitly so the comparison isn't overstated.

Alignment: benchmark returns computed at the same monthly panel dates as the strategy series via nearest-available-price-at-or-before lookups (`_pit_price`), 35 overlapping benchmark periods available within the strategies' invested windows (2022-2026) out of the possible 111 across the full panel — MASI's own price history did not fully span the entire panel window either, which further shortens the benchmark-comparable sample. This is reported as 35 periods in every strategy's `benchmark_periods` metric, not silently assumed to be the full sample.
