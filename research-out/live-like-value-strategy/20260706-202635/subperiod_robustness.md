# Subperiod Robustness (Phase 14)

One predeclared split: first half vs. second half of each strategy's invested window (not a searched breakpoint).

| Strategy | First-half Sharpe | Second-half Sharpe | First-half mean monthly return | Second-half mean monthly return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 B/M | 1.46 | 1.74 | 1.95% | 2.90% |
| S2 CF/P | 2.57 | 1.66 | 3.57% | 3.61% |
| S3 Composite | 2.26 | 1.50 | 3.20% | 2.80% |

**Direction persists in both halves for all three strategies** — no sign flip, no collapse to zero or negative in either half. Magnitude varies (S1 strengthens in the second half; S2 and S3 weaken somewhat but remain strongly positive in both halves). Given each half is only ~19-26 months, this is a reassuring-but-weak robustness signal — it rules out "the entire result is driven by one extreme half," but two ~20-month halves are each individually too short to be a strong robustness test on their own.
